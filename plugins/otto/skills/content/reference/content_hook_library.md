# Hook Library — Short-Form Video Openers

**Template Type:** Reference doc Otto pulls from when generating hook variants.
**The rule:** The first 3 seconds decide whether someone keeps watching. Hooks earn the rest of the video.

---

## Curiosity Hooks

People keep watching when they don't know what's coming next but want to.

**Patterns:**
- "The one thing nobody tells [audience] about [topic]..."
- "Here's what most [agents / buyers / sellers] get wrong about..."
- "I've sold [X] homes in [neighborhood]. They all had this in common."
- "There's a reason this house sold in [X] days and the one next door didn't."

**Examples:**
- "The one thing nobody tells first-time buyers about closing day."
- "Here's what most sellers get wrong about pricing in this market."
- "I've sold 30 homes in Travis Heights. They all had this in common."

---

## Contrarian Hooks

A clear, confident statement that goes against what most people think.

**Patterns:**
- "Stop [common advice]. Here's why."
- "Everyone tells you to [X]. That's outdated."
- "[Common belief] is wrong. Here's what's actually true."
- "[X] isn't [Y]. Let me explain."

**Examples:**
- "Stop waiting for rates to drop. Here's why."
- "Everyone tells you to put 20% down. That's outdated."
- "Open houses aren't dead. Most agents just run them wrong."

---

## Specific Number Hooks

Concrete numbers feel real and signal you've done the work.

**Patterns:**
- "I closed [X] deals last [time]. All [X] had this in common."
- "[X]% of [audience] do this. Don't be one of them."
- "Here are [X] things I check before [doing thing]."
- "[Specific number] reasons [thing] is happening right now in [market]."

**Examples:**
- "I closed 4 deals last month. All 4 had this in common."
- "82% of buyers skip this step. Don't be one of them."
- "Here are 3 things I check before recommending a price."

---

## Direct Callout Hooks

Speak directly to a specific audience. They self-select in the first second.

**Patterns:**
- "If you're trying to [do thing] in [area / situation] right now, watch this."
- "[Audience]: this one's for you."
- "Selling your home in [area] this [season / year]? Stop. Watch this first."
- "Buyers: if you're seeing [pattern], here's what's going on."

**Examples:**
- "If you're trying to buy in South Austin this summer, watch this."
- "Sellers: if your home's been on the market more than 30 days, this is for you."
- "Buyers — if every house you see needs $50K of work, here's why."

---

## Story Hooks

A one-sentence cold open that drops the viewer mid-story. They have to keep watching to know what happens.

**Patterns:**
- "My client almost lost their dream home over [small thing]."
- "I had to call my seller yesterday and tell them [unexpected thing]."
- "[X] days before closing, [thing] happened."
- "A buyer asked me a question last week I couldn't answer at first."

**Examples:**
- "My client almost lost their dream home over a $2,000 inspection issue."
- "I had to call my seller yesterday and tell them the buyer's loan fell through."
- "Three days before closing, the appraisal came in $30K short."

---

## Myth-Bust Hooks

Identify a thing "everyone knows" — then dismantle it.

**Patterns:**
- "[Common belief] is a myth."
- "If you've heard [X], that's not true anymore."
- "The biggest lie in real estate right now is [X]."
- "[X] used to be true. It isn't anymore."

**Examples:**
- "The 'spring is the best time to sell' rule is a myth in this market."
- "If you've heard 'wait until rates drop,' that's not the play this year."
- "The biggest lie in real estate right now is that the market is crashing."

---

## Behind-the-Scenes Hooks

People are curious about what agents actually do all day. Give them the look.

**Patterns:**
- "Here's what actually happens at a [listing appointment / showing / closing]."
- "Inside a real [event]: what you don't see."
- "POV: you're at a [event] in [area]."  *(use sparingly and only when literal)*
- "Walking into a [event]. Here's how I prep."

**Examples:**
- "Here's what actually happens at a listing appointment."
- "Inside a real bidding war: what the buyers never see."
- "Walking into a tough seller meeting. Here's how I prep."

---

## Question Hooks

A specific, sharp question — not a generic "Did you know?"

**Patterns:**
- "Why does [specific observation] happen?"
- "Ever wonder why [specific thing]?"
- "What's the difference between [X] and [Y]? Most agents get this wrong."

**Examples:**
- "Why do some listings sell in 3 days and others sit for months on the same street?"
- "Ever wonder why your offer keeps getting beat? Here's the pattern."

---

## What to Avoid as Hooks

- "Hey guys! Today I want to talk about..."
- "In today's market..."
- "As a real estate agent..."
- "Buying a home can be stressful..."
- "Have you ever thought about..."
- Any opener that takes more than 3 seconds to get to the point.

---

## Hook-Vibe Pairings

Use this as a guide when matching hooks to the vibe the agent picked:

| Vibe | Best Hook Types |
|---|---|
| Educational / informative | Specific number, curiosity, question |
| Conversational / casual | Story, behind-the-scenes, direct callout |
| Hot take / contrarian | Contrarian, myth-bust |
| Behind-the-scenes | Behind-the-scenes, story |
| Punchy / high-energy | Specific number, contrarian, direct callout |
| Match my established voice | Pull from across all categories, weight toward what's in their past content |
